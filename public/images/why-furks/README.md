> Create ONE standalone website illustration asset for Furks Coffee. The input

> object fresh; NEVER crop, extract, reproduce, or include any portion of the

> cream background, no backdrop circle (the circle will be CSS), no scene or

> The asset will display at 160–200px wide.

Ayrı varlık istemleri:

  textured medium-dark roasted coffee beans, viewed from a slightly overhead

  sacks or equipment.

  very fine engraved detail; twig from lower left to upper right. No pot or roots.

  Tactile, softly lit realistic painterly still life. Subtle handle, no saucer,

- **Coffee tamper:** One espresso tamper lying diagonally, rounded walnut handle

  naturally curved branching stems, large deeply veined forest green leaves,
