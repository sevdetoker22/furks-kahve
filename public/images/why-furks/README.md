# Neden Furks görselleri

Bu bölüm için yerleşik Imagegen aracıyla ayrı ayrı üretilmiş görsel varlıkları.
Kullanıcının onayladığı `E4E32D1E-863E-4609-AD80-ADB1F60E3D38.png`
yalnızca stil ve kompozisyon referansı olarak kullanıldı. Referans ekran
görüntüsünden herhangi bir parça kırpılmadı veya arka plan olarak kullanılmadı.

## Dosyalar

| Dosya | Kullanım |

| `roasted-beans.webp` | Sipariş üzerine kavrum |
| `coffee-branch.webp` | Şeffaf kaynak |

Görseller gerçek alfa şeffaflığıyla kaydedildi. Dört satır görseli 512 px,
dekoratif botanik görsel 1024 px WebP olarak optimize edildi. Krem daireler,
ayırıcılar, başlıklar, açıklamalar ve buton görsele gömülü değildir; HTML/CSS
ile oluşturulur. Dekoratif görseller erişilebilirlik ağacında gizlenir.

## Üretim istemleri

Ortak istem:

> Create ONE standalone website illustration asset for Furks Coffee. The input
> image is STYLE AND SUBJECT REFERENCE ONLY, not an edit target. Generate the
> object fresh; NEVER crop, extract, reproduce, or include any portion of the
> website screenshot. Match the reference's refined warm editorial coffee still
> lifes: natural tactile materials, muted forest green, warm coffee brown and
> ivory, soft upper-left daylight, detailed natural texture, restrained soft
> contact shadow. Isolated on a genuinely TRANSPARENT background, no baked-in
> cream background, no backdrop circle (the circle will be CSS), no scene or
> table, no lettering, no logo, no border, no watermark. Keep the entire object
> visible, centered with approximately 10% transparent padding, square composition.
> The asset will display at 160–200px wide.

Ayrı varlık istemleri:

- **Roasted beans:** A small casually scattered group of about twelve richly
  textured medium-dark roasted coffee beans, viewed from a slightly overhead
  three-quarter angle. Warm brown surfaces, visible natural center fissures,
  a few overlapping beans and a few separated smaller beans. No cup, leaves,
  sacks or equipment.
- **Coffee branch:** A graceful diagonal Coffea arabica twig, finely veined
  dark forest green leaves, ripe rust-red/brown coffee cherries and delicate
  white star-shaped blossoms. Vintage hand-painted botanical watercolor with
  very fine engraved detail; twig from lower left to upper right. No pot or roots.
- **Coffee cup:** One small handmade warm ivory stoneware cup filled with black
  coffee, viewed from a high three-quarter angle with a little golden crema.
  Tactile, softly lit realistic painterly still life. Subtle handle, no saucer,
  steam, beans or table.
- **Coffee tamper:** One espresso tamper lying diagonally, rounded walnut handle
  pointing upper left, circular brushed metal base facing the viewer toward
  lower right, a restrained scattering of finely ground coffee around the base.
  Natural realistic painterly material detail and soft shadow; no other equipment.
- **Botanical illustration:** An exquisite vintage Coffea arabica branch,
  naturally curved branching stems, large deeply veined forest green leaves,
  clusters of ripe reddish-brown cherries and delicate ivory blossoms. Fine
  antique copperplate engraving linework with hand-painted botanical watercolors.
  Organic diagonal foliage from lower left toward upper right, irregular
  silhouette and transparent negative space; no pot, label, frame or backdrop.
